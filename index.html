01 · Your first weeks

Getting started as a new ambassador

Start here if you were just accepted and haven't set anything up yet. This covers your first two to four weeks, roughly in the order things come up.

  • WTM Ambassador Onboarding Match Form

    Start here. About four minutes — your region, time zone, and working languages, so we can match you with a mentor or a small onboarding pod.

  • Welcome session — choose one

    Both sessions cover the same material and are recorded, so pick whichever fits your week: Session 1, Thu Sept 24, 15:00–16:00 UTC or Session 2, Tue Sept 29, 21:00–22:00 UTC.

  • Advocu

    Our system of record for logging contributions. Right now you just need to confirm you can sign in — nothing to log yet.

  • Getting Started Checklist

    The full Day 1–30 task list — schedule your first buddy call, review the safety escalation path, and see what triggers your Day-30 credential.

  • WTM Ambassador Introductions Deck

    Duplicate a format slide and introduce yourself: what you build, what you want to organize in your region this year, and what you want help with.

  • WTM Ambassador Impact Portfolio template

    Make your own copy in weeks 2–4 to keep a running, personal record of your activities and evidence alongside Advocu.

03 · Planning something local

Running events

When you're ready to plan and host something in your community, these are the tools and the rules of the road.

  • WTM public events calendar (Luma)

    Where public workshops, panels, and meetups are listed. Submit your own event here at least 14 days ahead of the date to qualify for official promotion in the newsletter and Slack.

  • Advocu

    Log your event once it's run — each co-organizer logs their own entry, since Advocu doesn't support shared entries.

  • Event run-of-show templateComing soon

    A starting structure for a workshop, panel, or study group, including timing and roles.

  • Caregiver-friendly event guideComing soon

    Practical steps for making an event workable for organizers and attendees with caregiving responsibilities.

  • Speaker briefing guideComing soon

    What to send a guest speaker ahead of time — format, audience, and accessibility expectations.

05 · Making your work count

Reporting and impact

Logging your contributions is what keeps your Ambassador status active and visible. Here's where that happens.

  • Advocu

    Log every contribution as you go — this is the only official record of your active status. Quarterly deadlines apply (for example, Q2 activity must be logged by July 15).

  • Advocu data backup extension (optional)

    Built by fellow Ambassador Julissa Rodriguez — download a personal backup copy of your own logged history. Optional, and not a replacement for your Impact Portfolio.

  • Day-30 Onboarding Survey

    Five short questions. Submitting it, alongside your checklist and Introductions Deck slide, triggers your Day-30 digital credential.
